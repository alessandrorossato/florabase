## Decision D1 and implementation

Under operator-approved UX-DATE-001 decision D1 ([`scope-v1.md`](../scope-v1.md#ux-date-001-decision-d1)),
a new Journal entry opens with Today preselected while preserving explicit choice of Another date or
Date unknown, stored partial-date precision, and draft stability:

- [`frontend/src/events/OccurrenceDateChoice.tsx`](../../../frontend/src/events/OccurrenceDateChoice.tsx):
  `initialChoice` defaults to `"today"` (keeping `"unset"` supported as an explicit prop). When
  `initialChoice === "today"` and the initial `value` equals the device-local calendar day
  (`dayValue(localDay())`), the radio group stays on `Today, YYYY-MM-DD`; any stored historical
  date opens as `Another date` with its stored precision (`day`, `month`, or `year`), and a stored
  `null` date opens as `Date unknown` when `initialChoice="unknown"` is passed for editing.
- [`frontend/src/events/EventJournal.tsx`](../../../frontend/src/events/EventJournal.tsx):
  `newEntryDateChoice` defaults to `"today"`. `blankForm(newEntryDateChoice)` initializes
  `occurredOn` synchronously to the device-local day when `newEntryDateChoice === "today"` so a
  newly opened composer is immediately valid and pristine, and `dismissEditor` compares against the
  captured `initialForm` and initial radio choice so closing an untouched new entry dismisses
  cleanly while changing the radio choice or typing a partial date triggers the discard confirmation.
